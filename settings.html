First-time setup
  1. Put the laptop and iPhone on the same network. Turning on the iPhone's Personal Hotspot and joining it from the laptop works in the car.
  2. Open the link in Safari. It warns that the connection isn't private (the certificate is made on this laptop): tap Show Details → visit this website.
  3. Tap Start sending motion and allow motion access.
  4. If Windows asks, allow Motion Cues on private and public networks.
